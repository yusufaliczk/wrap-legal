1. Who we are

Wrap (the “App”) is published by Rıdvan Kabak, Antalya, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, within the meaning of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

Contact for anything about privacy: yusufali.cezik@hotmail.com.

2. What Wrap does on your phone

Wrap helps you review your photo and video library, find duplicates and clutter, compress videos and photos, and clean up duplicate contacts. All of this runs on your device. Your photos, videos, their thumbnails, file names, captions, locations and other metadata, and your contacts are read only on your phone and are never uploaded to us or to any of our service providers.

Features such as Smart scan (finding duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, large or blurry items) work on your device and only make suggestions. Nothing is deleted, merged or compressed unless you choose it.

3. Permissions we ask for

  • Photos. Needed to show, sort, compress and delete the items you choose. You can grant full or limited access (iOS lets you pick which photos Wrap can see) and change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos. If your library uses iCloud Photos, iOS may download an original from iCloud when you view or compress it; this happens between your device and Apple, not us.
  • Contacts (optional). Used only to find contacts saved more than once and to merge or delete the ones you choose. Contact details never leave your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode (optional). Used by iOS to lock Wrap. We never receive biometric data; iOS only tells the App whether unlocking succeeded.
  • Notifications (optional). Lets us send at most one reminder a week when there is something new to clean up. Turn them off any time in Settings › Notifications.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) (optional). See section 7.

5. How we use data and our legal bases

  • To provide the App and Wrap Pro (subscription status, restore purchases, notifications you asked for) — performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • To keep the App stable and secure and to improve it (crash reports, usage analytics, remote settings) — our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). You can object at any time (section 10).
  • To measure our advertising with your advertising identifier — your consent given through the iOS tracking prompt (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1) explicit consent). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules and lawful requests — legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • To answer your messages — legitimate interests and, where relevant, contract.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Tracking and advertising

The first time you open Wrap, iOS asks whether the App may track you. Only if you tap Allow does the Meta SDK use your device's advertising identifier to measure which of our ads brought you to Wrap. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not available to us or to Meta. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

We do not show third-party ads in Wrap and we do not sell your personal data. Under some US state laws, using the advertising identifier for ad measurement may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out by not allowing tracking or by writing to us.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where data leaves the EEA, the UK or Türkiye, the transfer relies on the safeguards the law provides, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and, for Türkiye, the transfer conditions of KVKK Article 9.

9. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device (preferences, decisions, statistics, the in-app trash list): until you delete the App.
  • Usage analytics: up to 14 months, then deleted or kept only in aggregate form.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: while your subscription is active and afterwards as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes.
  • Push tokens: until they stop being valid (for example after you delete the App).
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after our last exchange.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: access your data and receive a copy; have it corrected; have it deleted; restrict or object to its use (including for analytics based on legitimate interests); data portability; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Türkiye (KVKK Article 11): you may learn whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly, know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, request correction or deletion and notification of these to third parties, object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

US states (such as California): you may request to know, delete or correct personal data and opt out of “sharing” for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To use any right, write to yusufali.cezik@hotmail.com. Because Wrap has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find your data (for example the date you installed the App or your purchase receipt). We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority — in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EU the authority of your country.

Deleting the App removes everything stored on your device. Cancelling a subscription is done in Apple's settings (see our Terms of Use).

11. Children

Wrap is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA where local law requires) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data sent to our providers travels encrypted (TLS) and is stored by them with industry-standard safeguards. Your library and contacts stay on your device and are protected by iOS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will inform you and the authorities of any breach as the law requires.
