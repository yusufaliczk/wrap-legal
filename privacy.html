Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short

Wrap processes your photos, videos and contacts only on your phone. None of them is ever uploaded to us or to any other server.

Library and contacts

The access you grant is used only to scan your library, show your items, compress them, and delete or merge the ones you choose. Every deletion and merge goes through iOS's own confirmation. Deleted items stay in the Recently Deleted album in Photos for 30 days.

What we collect

To improve the app we collect usage events tied to a random app ID, never to your name (such as which screen was opened or how many decisions were made), crash reports, and technical details such as device model, iOS version and language. None of this contains photo, video or contact content. We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging) and Mixpanel for this.

Ad measurement

Only if you allow it (App Tracking Transparency), the Meta SDK may use your device's advertising identifier to measure how our ads perform. If you don't allow it, the identifier is not used, and you can change your choice in Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Your subscription is verified through RevenueCat with a random, device-specific ID.

Notifications

If you allow them, we may remind you at most once a week when there is something new to clean up. You can turn notifications off in Settings at any time.

Retention and deletion

Your preferences and decisions are kept only on your phone; deleting the app deletes them too. You can ask us to delete the usage data tied to your app ID by writing to yusufali.cezik@hotmail.com.

Children

Wrap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

Wrap is published by Rıdvan Kabak, Antalya, Türkiye. If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page. For any question write to yusufali.cezik@hotmail.com or use Profile › Contact us in the app.
