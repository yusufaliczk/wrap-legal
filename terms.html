4. How cleaning works — please read

  • You decide. Wrap only suggests. Photos, videos and contacts are deleted, merged or compressed only after you choose them and confirm in iOS's own dialog. Smart scan, duplicate and “similar” suggestions are automatic and may be wrong — check them before you confirm.
  • Wrap's trash. Items you swipe away first go to Wrap's own trash list, where you can restore them. Emptying it asks iOS to delete them.
  • Recently Deleted. Photos and videos deleted by iOS move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos and are removed permanently by iOS after about 30 days, or sooner if you empty that album.
  • iCloud Photos. If iCloud Photos is on, deleting an item removes it from all your devices that use the same library.
  • Compression. Compressing creates a new, smaller copy in your library and then, after your confirmation, deletes the original (to Recently Deleted). The copy has lower quality by design; some metadata may not be preserved. Items that would not get smaller are skipped.
  • Contacts. Merging or deleting contacts changes your address book directly. Contacts have no Recently Deleted folder: a deleted or merged-away contact cannot be restored from Wrap. If your contacts sync with iCloud or another account, the change syncs too (iCloud.com may let you restore an earlier version of your contacts).
  • Back up. Keep your own backup (for example iCloud or a computer) of anything important before cleaning.
  • Space freed figures are estimates; the space iOS actually frees depends on iCloud, Recently Deleted and other system factors.
